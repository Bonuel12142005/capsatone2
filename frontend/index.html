About EchoTrace

Smart Shopping Safeguarded by AI Audits

Online marketplaces are flooded with manipulated, sponsored, and AI-generated feedback. EchoTrace aims to bring transparency back to e-commerce using Natural Language Processing.

1. NLP Processing

Reviews are scraped directly from the store, cleaned of metadata noise, and analyzed for structural anomalies and spam triggers.

2. AI Risk Prediction

Our advanced Natural Language Processing model evaluates reviewer sentiment, emotional biases, toxicity, and grammar quality.

3. Duplication Cluster

Detects copy-pasted seller spam or coordinate campaign reviews by mapping identical phrases across duplicate groups.

Our Vision & Core Objectives

We believe that honest shopper reviews are crucial for safe digital commerce. By leveraging automated classification algorithms, EchoTrace empowers buyers to spot warning signs and bad actors instantly.

  • Mitigate paid spam review campaigns
  • Expose bots and AI auto-generated content
  • Track sentiment patterns and duplicate clusters
