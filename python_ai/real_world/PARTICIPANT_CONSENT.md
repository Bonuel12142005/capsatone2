## 1. Purpose of the Research
This academic project evaluates how well EchoTrace's existing machine-learning model detects suspicious or fake ecommerce reviews across English, Tagalog, and Taglish.

## 2. What Data Will Be Collected
- Publicly visible or user-donated ecommerce review text (`review_text`), platform name (`platform`), product category (`product_category`), language (`language`), and collection date (`collection_date`).
- **Not Collected:** Customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, account IDs, order IDs, or residential addresses.

## 3. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw
- Participation is strictly voluntary.
- You may withdraw your donated review records at any time **before** the anonymized records are finalized into the locked evaluation batch by contacting `mckenllyhuertas@gmail.com` with your `[CONSENT_REF_ID]`.

## 4. Participant Rights & Platform Terms
